## Purpose

To define minimum specifications and requirements for SLS radios used in SA.

## Overview

Only SLSSA approved radio makes and models listed in the SLSSA Coastal Radio Network Approved Equipment List are to be utilised for lifesaving, training and sport operations to ensure compatibility and reliable operation within the SLSSA Coastal Radio Network.

Radios for lifesaving operations must be purchased only from SLSSA approved suppliers/dealers via the Surf Life Saving SA Portal at [www.surflifesavingsa.com.au/portal](http://www.surflifesavingsa.com.au/portal).

SLS services must not obtain quotes directly from suppliers. Requests for quotes must be made via the Surf Life Saving SA Portal.

Only SLSSA approved radio service agents are authorised to service or program SLS radios.

SLS radios must only have the SLSSA schedule of radio frequencies/channels programmed into them, unless approved by the Manager, Technologies and State Operations Centre.

SLS personnel must not provide SLSSA frequencies to external parties, unless authorised by the Manager, Technologies and State Operations Centre.

Non-SLS radios must not be programmed with SLSSA frequencies without written approval from the Manager, Technologies and State Operations Centre.

External services authorised to use SLSSA frequencies must reapply annually.

SLS services must service all radio equipment regularly, including frequency/channel alignment, as outlined in [SOP 7.3 – Radio Equipment Maintenance and Servicing](../7-communications/7.3-radio-equipment-maintenance-and-servicing.md).

Programming, including special functions, must be conducted in accordance with directions from the Manager, Technologies and State Operations Centre.

SLS services must not operate other radio systems (e.g. VHF Marine, 27 MHz) without authorisation from the Manager, Technologies and State Operations Centre.

## Definitions

- **Base/Mobile:** Fixed radio unit usually located in towers, SLSCs or vehicles.
- **Portable/Handheld:** Radio units used by individuals.
- **Lifesaving Operations:** Patrolling, emergency response, training, events and carnivals.

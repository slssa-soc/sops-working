## Purpose

To define standard radio zone and channel allocations used for SLS communications in SA.

## Procedure

All SLS radios operating within the SLSSA Coastal Radio Network utilise the following zone and channel assignments.

## Definitions

- **Simplex:** Direct radio to radio communications without a repeater.
- **Repeater:** A networked radio channel designed to extend communications coverage.
- **Zone:** A group of channels.

### Zones

A zone is a collection of channels.

- **Zone A –** Normal patrol operations and includes the main channels used by SLS services.
- **Zone B –** Manual selection of a repeater channel, usually only used at the direction of the State Operations Centre for troubleshooting.
- **Zone C –** Surf Sports.

Zone A should be utilised for standard patrol operations.

### Channel Priority

The channels in Zones A and B are primarily for SLS operations. Where channels are not being used for operational activities, they may be utilised for training in accordance with [SOP 7.9 – Radio Training](7.9-radio-training.md).

### Temporary Change to Channel Usage in Chiton Rocks and Normanville Areas

In the Chiton Rocks and Normanville areas, Zone A – Channel 2 must be utilised as the primary patrol channel.

## Purpose

To outline procedures for responding to inappropriate, suspicious, aggressive, or anti-social behaviour encountered during patrol operations.

## Overview

SLS personnel may encounter behaviour that presents a risk to public safety, member welfare, or patrol operations, including aggressive, suspicious, offensive, intoxicated, or anti-social behaviour.

SLS personnel must prioritise personal safety, member welfare, and continuation of lifesaving operations at all times.

Where required, assistance should be requested through the [State Operations Centre (SOC)](../12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.2-state-operations-centre-soc-surfcom.md).

## Procedure

### Response to Inappropriate or Suspicious Behaviour

Where inappropriate, suspicious, aggressive, or anti-social behaviour is reported or observed, SLS personnel should:

- Maintain personal safety and avoid unnecessary confrontation,
- Maintain a safe distance from the individual or incident,
- Consider restricting public access to the affected area where safe and practicable,
- Observe and record relevant information including descriptions, location, involved vehicles, or behaviour,
- Obtain information from witnesses where appropriate,
- Notify the SOC,
- Maintain communications with patrol personnel and the SOC,
- Continue patrol surveillance and lifesaving operations where practicable, and
- Escalate any immediate threats to emergency services.

Where safe to do so, personnel may continue to monitor the situation from a safe location while awaiting emergency services or additional assistance.

Operational surveillance, patrol capability, and minimum lifesaving service standards must be maintained wherever practicable during the management of behavioural incidents.

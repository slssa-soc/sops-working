## Purpose

To outline governance, operational equipment, and compliance requirements for Side by Side Vehicles (SSVs) utilised within SLS operations.

## Overview

Side by Side Vehicles (SSVs), formerly referred to as All-Terrain Vehicles (ATVs), provide SLS services with rapid beach mobility and operational response capability.

Operational beach driving procedures and deployment requirements are detailed within [SOP 8.6 – Beach Vehicle Operations](../8-patrol-operations-general/8.6-beach-vehicle-operations.md).

All SSVs must comply with:

- Applicable legislation,
- Manufacturer operating requirements,
- SLSA equipment specifications,
- Organisational procedures, and
- Local operating requirements.

Single-seat quad bikes must not be utilised for SLS operations.

## Procedure

### Operator Requirements

SSV operators must:

- Hold a current and valid C Class driver licence (provisional or full),
- Be authorised SLS personnel,
- Complete induction training for the specific vehicle,
- Be competent to operate the vehicle safely, and
- Only operate SSVs for approved operational purposes.

Where an SSV is operated by a provisional licence holder, P plates must be displayed in accordance with applicable South Australian legislation and regulations.

Operator inductions should be recorded by the relevant club or service.

### Vehicle Standards and Compliance

All SSVs must:

- Meet SLSA approved gear and equipment specifications,
- Be maintained in a safe operational condition,
- Be appropriately registered where required,
- Comply with applicable legislation and manufacturer requirements, and
- Be fitted with required safety equipment.

Any modifications including storage systems, roll bars, communications equipment, lighting, or carry racks must comply with manufacturer requirements and applicable legislation.

Single-seat quad bikes are not approved for SLS operations and may invalidate organisational insurance coverage.

### Operational Equipment

Operational SSVs should carry:

- Rescue board,
- Rescue tube and fins,
- Radio communications equipment,
- Loud hailer or PA system,
- Side doors or barriers where fitted,
- First aid equipment where operationally required, and
- Other operational equipment appropriate to the service role.

### Lighting and Warning Devices

SSVs should operate with headlights activated while in motion where practicable.

Amber warning lights:

- Must be hard wired,
- Must comply with applicable legislation, and
- Should be operated where increased visibility is required.

Sirens must not be utilised on public roads or carriageways and should only be used for approved operational purposes.

Use of warning lights or sirens does not exempt operators from complying with applicable legislation or operational requirements.

### Vehicle Branding

Branding and identification of SSVs must comply with the SLSA Lifesaving Equipment and Uniform Branding Guidelines.

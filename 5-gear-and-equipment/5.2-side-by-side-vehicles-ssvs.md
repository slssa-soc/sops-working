## Purpose

To outline requirements for Side by Side (SSVs) in Surf Life Saving operations.

## Overview

Side by Side (SSV), formerly known as All-Terrain Vehicles (ATVs), enable suitably qualified SLS personnel to be more mobile and capable of responding to emergencies both inside and outside of their patrolled area (on sand).

## Procedure

All SSV oeprators must:

- Hold a valid C Class driver licence (provisional or full), and
- Be authorised SLS personnel undertaking approved duties.

All SSV operators must be inducted in the operation of the specific SSV by a nominated SLSC/service officer.

All SSVs are to meet SLSA gear and equipment specifications. These specifications are outlined in the [SLSA Approved Gear and Equipment List](https://members.sls.com.au/members/document_library/1/media/12129).

Single Seat (Quad Bikes) must not be used by members for SLS operations. Any use of ‘quads’ forfeits coverage by the association’s insurance policy for any member/club/service involved in an incident.

SLS personnel must comply all applicable laws and regulations relating to vehicles (including registration, speed, seatbelts, and parking) at all times.

SSV operators must comply with all speed limits and operate at a safe speed appropriate to the environment and conditions.

SLS personnel are not exempt from any laws, regulations, or by-laws.

Operators and passengers must comply with council by-laws, Road Traffic Act and Australian Road Rules, for example seat belts. It is the driver’s responsibility to evaluate the environment to determine a safe and appropriate speed within these limits.

Passengers should not exceed maximums set within the SSV owner/operator manual.

SSVs should operate with headlights on when in motion.

Amber lights must be hard wired and operated whenever the vehicle is running.

Use of lights does not exempt SLS personnel from complying with any applicable laws, regulations, or by-laws.

SSVs must comply with all relevant vehicle standards and legislation.

### Registration

All SSVs must be registered in accordance with applicable state requirements and SLSSA processes.

### Rescue Equipment

All on-duty SSVs should carry the following lifesaving equipment and hold the following safety items:

- Rescue board Rescue tube & fins,
- Radio,
- Loud Hailer/PA System, and
- Side doors/barriers.

SSVs operators must not use a siren while operating on public roads or carriageways. Sirens are strictly for beach use only.

Any modifications including roll bars, carry racks and storage containers must adhere to manufacturer’s guidelines and be carried out in consultation with the manufacturer or dealer.

### Vehicle Branding

Branding for all SLS SSVs must comply with the SLSA Lifesaving Equipment and Uniform Branding Guidelines.

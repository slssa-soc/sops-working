## Purpose

To provide operational guidance for SLS support activities during tsunami warnings, tsunami response operations, and coastal evacuation activities.

## Overview

SLS personnel remain under SLS command arrangements while supporting emergency service or Control Agency operations.

SLS personnel and facilities may be exposed to elevated risk during tsunami events due to their proximity to coastal environments and operational activities conducted within shoreline areas.

SLS may assist with public warning, evacuation support, shoreline observation, communications, and approved support operations where tasked or operationally required.

## Procedure

### Operational Coordination

SLS support activities during tsunami warnings or tsunami events may include:

- Shoreline observation and monitoring,
- Public warning support,
- Evacuation support activities,
- Communications support,
- Coastal reconnaissance,
- Welfare support activities, and
- Other approved support operations as tasked.

All tasking and deployment requests should occur through the State Operations Centre (SOC).

### Immediate Response Actions

Where tsunami warnings or tsunami related coastal impacts occur:

- Patrol personnel should monitor official warnings and operational advice,
- Supervised swimming operations should be suspended where required,
- Patrol flags should be removed where evacuation or closure actions are implemented,
- Beach users should be advised to leave coastal areas and move to higher ground where directed by emergency warnings or emergency services,
- Members should remain clear of hazardous shoreline areas, and
- The State Operations Centre (SOC) should be updated regarding operational impacts and local observations.

### Shoreline Observation

SLS services should remain aware of unusual coastal conditions which may indicate tsunami activity, including:

- Rapid water retreat,
- Sudden changes in sea level,
- Unusual currents or wave activity,
- Significant surging water movement, or
- Unusual ocean behaviour.

Any significant observations should be reported to the State Operations Centre (SOC) as soon as reasonably practicable.

### Public Safety

Where safe and operationally appropriate, SLS personnel may assist with public warnings and evacuation messaging.

Members should not place themselves at risk to conduct warnings, evacuations, or shoreline observations.

Operational priorities should include:

- Preservation of life,
- Member safety,
- Rapid evacuation from hazardous coastal areas, and
- Coordination with emergency services and Control Agency arrangements.

### Post Incident Operations

Operational actions, warnings, evacuations, and significant events should be documented in accordance with organisational reporting requirements.

Where required, SLS may assist with approved post incident support operations under coordinated tasking arrangements.

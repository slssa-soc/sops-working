## Purpose

To provide operational guidance regarding the use of the Operations App for patrol operations, incident recording, and operational reporting.

## Overview

SLS services:

- Must use the Operations App to record regular patrol functions (sign on, sign off and vehicle availability),
- Must use the Operations App to record incidents,
- May utilise Operations App functionality to support IRB operational logging requirements., and
- Must prioritise the use of the Operations App for patrol (e.g. when water cover is occurring and a regular patrol is rostered, the Operations App must exclusively be used by the patrol).

## Procedure

### Operational Use

Operational information entered into the Operations App should be accurate, timely, and completed as soon as reasonably practicable.

Information entered into the Operations App may be monitored by the SOC and other SLS services to support operational awareness and incident coordination.

### Patrol Reporting

1. If a SLSC is unable to commence patrol as rostered, but have resources available, they should sign on with a _‘None’_ or _‘Setup’_ patrol status.
2. Patrol statistics should be updated at the start of patrol, mid patrol and end of patrol.
3. The notes section must be used to provide the SOC with supplementary operational information e.g. patrol location (essential information must be communicated to the SOC by radio or phone).

### Photo of the Day

4. The Photo of The Day must show the beach conditions and will automatically be added to Beachsafe.

### Business Continuity

Where the Operations App is unavailable, patrol operations should continue using approved contingency arrangements (paper log) and records must be entered retrospectively.

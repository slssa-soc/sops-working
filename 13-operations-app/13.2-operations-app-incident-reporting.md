## Purpose

To provide operational guidance regarding incident notification, coordination, and incident recording within the Operations App.

## Overview

SLS services must:

- Notify the [State Operations Centre](../12-state-operations-centre-soc-surfcom/12.1-state-operations-centre-soc-surfcom.md) of incidents which should be logged,
- Use the Operations App to record incidents,
- Follow the direction of the State Operations Centre for how incidents should be recorded, to ensure accurate statistics, and
- Make best efforts to ensure they do not duplicate statistics.

The SOC does not need to be notified of minor injuries unless operational support, escalation, or incident logging is required.

Where an SLS service assists another SLS service, the incident should only be recorded once unless otherwise directed by the SOC.

The SOC maintains oversight of operational incident records and coordination activities.

## Procedure

### Incident Notification

Surf clubs and services must notify the State Operations Centre by radio or phone when an incident occurs and operational logging, coordination, escalation, or support is required.

The SOC will determine whether an incident record is required and provide direction regarding how the incident should be recorded.

### Incident Creation

The SOC will create an incident in the incident management system and add relevant details during the incident.

At the conclusion of the incident, the SOC will notify the patrol that the incident has been closed within the incident management system, which will make the incident log available to the patrol in the Operations App.

### Operations App Recording

The patrol must open the incident in the Operations App by navigating to:

**Operations App > Menu > Incidents**

The patrol must add relevant operational and patient information as required.

Incident information should be completed as soon as reasonably practicable following the incident.

### Follow-Up Information

Authorised personnel may reopen the incident in the Operations App to add follow-up information.

Significant incident updates must be communicated to the SOC by phone.

### Duplicate Records

SLS services must make best efforts to avoid duplicate incident records.

Where multiple SLS services are involved in the same incident, the SOC will provide direction regarding which service should complete or update the incident record.
